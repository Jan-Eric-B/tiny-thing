# 05: Artwork, Backdrop and Accent

**What to build:** The Now Playing screen with Artwork, Backdrop and Accent. The Tray core turns the Media session's image into Artwork at the exact Device pixel size, a Backdrop and an Accent color, identifies the Artwork by a hash of its content, and sends an image only when the Device does not already have it. The Device app lays out the Artwork beside white text on the Backdrop and never rescales images.

**Blocked by:** 01

**Status:** in-progress

- [x] Downscaling with Lanczos3 in linear light; upscaling with Mitchell in sRGB, capped at 2x, smaller drawing beyond that
- [x] Non-square Artwork (16:9 thumbnails) is shown whole and undistorted
- [x] Backdrop: shrink to about 32 px (box), enlarge (Gaussian), darken until its brightest pixel has at least 4.5:1 contrast to white
- [x] Accent: color from the Artwork adjusted to at least 4.5:1 contrast against the Backdrop
- [x] Seam 1 tests: received images have the exact expected size; the Artwork id is stable for identical content and changes when only the image changes (the Tidal case: new title first, new image a moment later); Backdrop and Accent contrast hold for a set of test images including very bright, very dark and mixed covers
- [x] The Device keeps the last few images and the Tray core does not resend them
- [x] Images travel lossless
- [x] Text is white and never drawn over the Artwork

## Comments

**2026-11-23, implementation.**

- Protocol (version 4): `NowPlaying` carries `artwork: Option<ArtworkId>`; `ArtworkId(u64)` is a hash of the image bytes the player handed out, never of title or artist. `Artwork { id, image, backdrop, accent: Rgb }` travels as `TrayMessage::Artwork` and `DaemonMessage::Artwork`; the Device asks for Artwork it does not have with `DeviceMessage::MissingArtwork(id)`, and the Tray app sends Artwork only on request. `protocol::Image` is a lossless QOI image (RGB, `qoi` 0.4.1) with `encode`/`decode`/`size`; cheap to clone (`Arc<[u8]>`), serialized as a byte string, logged as its size only, and `decode` refuses more than `MAX_IMAGE_SIDE` (2048) a side so a hostile header cannot make the Device allocate much. `SCREEN_WIDTH`/`SCREEN_HEIGHT` (800x480) and `ARTWORK_BOX` (352) live in the protocol as the shared size contract.
- Tray core: `media::NowPlaying` has `artwork: Option<media::Artwork>` (the encoded image as the player supplies it; the id is computed once when it is made, with the std hasher, which is fixed within a build: a new Tray app release at most misses the Device's kept Artwork once). The pipeline (`tray-core/src/artwork.rs`, pure): decode with `image` 0.25.10 (BMP, JPEG, PNG, WebP; at most 8192 a side), scale with `fast_image_resize` 6.2.0 to `ARTWORK_BOX` on the longer side, at most 2x (`artwork_size`): smaller with Lanczos3 in linear light (sRGB mapped to 16-bit linear and back), larger with Mitchell in sRGB. Backdrop: the middle of the Artwork in 5:3 box-filtered down to 40x24 and Gaussian-enlarged to 800x480 (both in linear light), then all pixels scaled by one factor in linear light (rounded down) until the brightest has 4.5:1 to white; an already dark Backdrop stays as it is. Accent: the most colorful of the 40x24 colors, made lighter in the same hue (strongest channel to full), then towards white, until it is lighter than the Backdrop's brightest pixel by 4.5:1, so it stands out on every pixel. In the session, Now Playing goes out at once with the id; `ArtworkSupply` prepares the Artwork only when the Device asks, one preparation at a time on `spawn_blocking`, keeps the prepared current one, and drops results for Artwork no longer playing, so the message loop and Now Playing never wait for images.
- Device daemon: keeps the last `ARTWORK_CACHE_LEN` (4) Artworks (least recently used goes first; a few MB at most), across Tray app connections, and asks the Tray app once per Artwork it does not have. The Artwork of the current Now Playing is handed to every new Device app connection; one it already has is not sent again.
- Windows adapter (`tray-app/src/windows_media/artwork.rs`): reads `MediaProperties.Thumbnail()` through `OpenReadAsync` and a `DataReader` with the 2 s read limit, at most 16 MB; a thumbnail that cannot be read is left out (debug log) rather than holding up the text. Needs the `Storage_Streams` feature of `windows`.
- Device app: the Artwork sits in a 352x352 box left of the text column (32 px margins; text column 352 px, the three controls fill it), drawn at its own pixel size, centered; the Backdrop is an 800x480 image behind everything; the control icons take the Accent. The Artwork, Backdrop and Accent show only when Now Playing names the id of the Artwork received last, so the previous song's Artwork never shows with the next song (a faint placeholder box and black ground until the new one arrives). Images are decoded on the UI thread when they arrive (once per Artwork). `fake-now-playing` takes `--artwork <file>`.
- Tests: protocol (images decode to the exact pixels, garbage and oversized headers refused, round trips), Device daemon (asked once, kept Artwork not asked again, also across Tray app connections, only the last 4 kept, late Device app gets the Artwork), Seam 1 (exact sizes for square, 16:9, portrait, small and tiny Artwork incl. JPEG; 16:9 shown whole; linear-light downscaling with a checkerboard; same id for the same image under any title; the Tidal case; unreadable Artwork does not stop Now Playing; white-text and Accent contrast on every Backdrop pixel for 11 covers from white to black, saturated, mixed and noise; Accent hue from the Artwork; dark Backdrop not darkened; Tray core sends Artwork only on request), Seam 3 (Artwork and Backdrop at their own pixel size, Artwork box is `ARTWORK_BOX`, text never overlaps the Artwork, late Artwork shown, no previous-song Artwork, kept Artwork shown again, no box without Artwork). The pipeline tests were checked against mutations (sRGB downscaling, no darkening, no Accent adjustment, no 2x cap, no id check): each makes a test fail.

Verified by hand (debug builds, Simulator with `SLINT_SCALE_FACTOR=1`): `fake-now-playing --artwork` with a 640x360 PNG showed it at 352x198 beside the text on its Backdrop; the real Tray app read TIDAL's thumbnail (49 KB) through the Windows adapter, and the Simulator showed it at 300x300, below the 352 box as the 2x cap requires for a small thumbnail, on a matching Backdrop about 0.4 s later. Not yet verified by hand: a song change in TIDAL (new title first, then the new image), and the look on a real Device (12).

Open question for the product: the Backdrop is darkened only as far as white text needs, so on bright or mid-tone covers its brightest spot reaches about sRGB gray 118, and the Accent then has to be near-white to get 4.5:1 (white, black-and-white, yellow, red and most mixed test covers give an Accent within a few steps of white; only dark covers keep a clearly colored one, such as 108,119,255 for a dark blue cover). For a visibly colored Accent the Backdrop would have to be darkened further than the 4.5:1-to-white minimum (for example until a mid-saturation Accent reaches 4.5:1), which the spec allows ("at least") but which changes the look. Also: in the Simulator on a scaled Windows display the window is scaled as a whole, so images are only pixel-exact with `SLINT_SCALE_FACTOR=1`; on the Device the scale is 1.

**2026-11-23, review fixes.** These supersede the implementation comment above where they differ.

- Protocol version 5. `protocol::Artwork` checks itself: private fields, `Artwork::new(id, image, backdrop, accent) -> Result<_, ArtworkError>` and a deserialize through the same check (`#[serde(try_from)]`). From the QOI headers alone, the Artwork must be 1 to `ARTWORK_BOX` pixels a side and the Backdrop exactly `SCREEN_WIDTH` x `SCREEN_HEIGHT`, so neither side ever decodes more than a screen's worth and the Artwork always fits its box. `MAX_IMAGE_SIDE` and `ImageError::TooLarge` are gone. A Tray app that sends a non-conforming Artwork has its message refused as unreadable: the Device daemon ends that connection (logged as a frame error; postcard does not carry the reason) before anything is kept. A const assertion checks that the worst-case Artwork message (both images at 4 bytes a pixel plus overhead, about 2 MB) fits `MAX_FRAME_LEN`. `Image::decode_into` decodes straight into a caller's buffer; the Device app decodes into Slint's `SharedPixelBuffer` with it.
- `TrayMessage::ArtworkUnavailable(id)` and `DaemonMessage::ArtworkUnavailable(id)`: when the Tray core cannot prepare the Artwork it was asked for, the Device app shows the song without Artwork (no box, text across the full width) instead of an empty box forever. The Device daemon remembers it per Tray app connection, so it is not asked again for that song and a Device app that starts later is told too; an Artwork that arrives after all is still shown. A received Artwork the Device app cannot decode counts as unavailable as well.
- Tray core: `ArtworkSupply` (own module `artwork_supply.rs`) takes the preparer as a function, keeps `Option<(ArtworkId, JoinHandle)>` and awaits the handle in the session's select loop, so a panicking preparation arrives as a `JoinError` (error log, answered as unavailable) and the next song's Artwork is still prepared. It remembers the outcome (ready or unavailable) of the current Artwork, so a repeated request is answered without preparing again. Requests from the Device go through a bounded queue (`ARTWORK_REQUEST_QUEUE`, 4, `try_send`, dropped with a debug log when full), in the session and likewise in the Device daemon. `media::Artwork::from_encoded` now returns `Result<_, UnreadableArtwork>`: it reads the image header (format guess and size, no decoding) and refuses images of 0 or more than 4096 pixels a side, so Now Playing names an id only for an image that looks readable. The id hashes the source bytes together with `PIPELINE_VERSION` (1).
- Pipeline: sources are capped at 4096 pixels a side, with `image::Limits::max_alloc` at 64 MiB (4096 x 4096 x 4 bytes), for a peak of about 150 MB; a decoded image without pixels is refused before any scaling. The source is converted to 16-bit linear light once; the Artwork (when made smaller) and the Backdrop both come from that copy.
- Accent and Backdrop (product decision of 2026-11-23: darken the Backdrop further when needed, so that a real, mid-saturation Accent still reaches 4.5:1). The Accent is the hue of the most colorful of the 40x24 blurred colors at full brightness and HSV saturation 0.5, made paler only until its luminance is at least 0.55 (white when that color is grayish: channels less than 24 apart). The icons sit on the controls' white circle, so the Accent's contrast is now measured against every Backdrop pixel with the pressed circle's white (`PRESSED_CONTROL_WHITE_ALPHA`, 0x40, blended in sRGB values) on top, not against the bare Backdrop. The Backdrop is then darkened by one factor in linear light (found by bisection over all pixels) until white text has 4.5:1 on every pixel and the Accent has 4.5:1 on every pressed-circle pixel; one that has both already stays as it is. `app.slint` takes the tint from a `Theme` global, and a Seam 3 test keeps it equal to the protocol constant. Result for the test covers: red covers give an Accent of about 255,175,175, blue about 184,192,255, yellow 255,245,128, gray and white covers white; every hue keeps an HSV saturation of at least 0.26. The cost: with the pressed circle's white on top even a white Accent needs the Backdrop's brightest pixel at about sRGB 72 or below (gray covers), and a colored Accent on a bright cover needs it at about 22 (a white cover with a red square, half white and half blue), where it was 118 before. Dark and saturated covers keep visibly colored Backdrops (for example 74,2,2 for a saturated red cover).
- Tests: protocol (`tests/artwork.rs`: Artwork within the box on a screen-sized Backdrop is made; larger Artwork, a 20000x20000 header and any other Backdrop size are refused with the specific `ArtworkError`; unreadable images are refused; a non-conforming Artwork cannot be read from a message; `decode_into` checks the buffer size; the old oversized-header test, which passed without its guard, is gone). Device daemon (unavailable reaches the Device app, also a restarted one and when the song comes back; a non-conforming Artwork never reaches the Device app or the kept Artworks). Seam 3 (unavailable shows as no Artwork with the text at its full-width position; word about another id leaves the box waiting; Artwork arriving after the word is shown; the pressed tint equals the protocol constant; text never overlaps an Artwork of the box's full size, 352x1 or 1x352; `the_previous_songs_artwork_is_not_shown_with_the_next_song` is now `artwork_shows_only_with_the_now_playing_that_names_its_id`). `ArtworkSupply` unit tests with a stand-in preparer the test lets finish (ready, failing or panicking): answered once and kept; a result for Artwork no longer playing is dropped and the current one prepared next; a failure, a panic, and a panic for Artwork no longer playing each leave the next song's Artwork working; a request for Artwork no longer playing starts nothing. Pipeline unit test: a source of 0x0, 0x300 or 300x0 pixels is refused. Seam 1: garbage, a 0x0 header and a 4097-pixel-wide image are no Artwork; a PNG cut off halfway gets an id and reaches the Device app as unavailable, and the next song still gets its Artwork; the Accent has 4.5:1 on a pressed control on every Backdrop pixel of the 11 covers; eight colored covers give an Accent of HSV saturation at least 0.25 in the cover's hue; gray covers give white; the dark-cover test uses 10,20,45 (20,40,90 now needs darkening for its Accent). Mutation-checked: `start()` removed from the finish path, failures not answered, the Accent measured on the bare Backdrop, a near-white Accent, no darkening, the Device app ignoring unavailable, a different tint in `app.slint`, no checked deserialize, no Artwork size check, the Device daemon forgetting unavailable: each makes a test fail.
- Windows adapter (tray-app, done in parallel): Now Playing goes out at once with the Artwork already known for the song (none for a new song), and again once the thumbnail is read, so play/pause and title changes do not wait for the thumbnail. The thumbnail is read again only when the Media session changes or the player reports `MediaPropertiesChanged`; a play/pause reuses the last Artwork. A changed title, artist, album artist or album title counts as a new song (`AlbumTitle` is now read). In the Tidal case a second `MediaPropertiesChanged` under the same title still reads the new image, and the previous image stays until it is read. A thumbnail that cannot be read (error, timeout or truncated: fewer bytes loaded than the stream size) keeps the previous Artwork of the same song and is read again on the next wake-up, with no retry timer. Reading (open plus load) is limited to 1 s in total, and a timed-out step is cancelled in Windows (this replaces the 2 s per step above). The rules are pure logic in `tray-app/src/windows_media/known_artwork.rs` with 10 unit tests. A thumbnail that is no readable image (`from_encoded` refuses it) counts as no Artwork. Checked by hand against TIDAL: on a title change the text went out first and the Artwork 216 ms later; a pause reused the Artwork.

Not changed: `serde_bytes` instead of the hand-written byte-string visitor for `Image`. It does not take `Arc<[u8]>`, so it would need a conversion as well, which is not simpler. Still to verify by hand: the look on a real Device (12), and how dark the Backdrops of bright covers look there.

**2026-11-23, open question for the product.** The 4.5:1 for the Accent on a pressed control is what makes bright covers' Backdrops so dark: the circle's white lifts even black to about sRGB 64, so a colored Accent needs a nearly black Backdrop around it. Options that would let Backdrops stay brighter: a pressed state that does not lighten the circle (for example a darker circle or a ring), 3:1 for the icons (WCAG's minimum for graphics rather than text), or a lighter, paler Accent (raising `ACCENT_MIN_LUMINANCE` from 0.55 to 0.75 allows a brightest Backdrop pixel of about 48 instead of 22, at a saturation of about 0.13 for blue).
